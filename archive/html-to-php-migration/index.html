More Than A Chef

A Storyteller Through Food

Food has the power to bring people together, create memories and tell stories. For Chef Ched Njogu, cooking is more than preparing a meal — it is about transforming quality ingredients into experiences that people remember.

Over the past seven years, Ched has developed his craft through hands-on culinary experience, continuous learning and a passion for creating food that combines flavour, presentation and personality.

The Culinary Philosophy

Simple Ingredients. Bold Ideas. Exceptional Experiences.

  • Ched believes great food starts with quality ingredients and is elevated through technique, creativity and attention to detail. His cooking is inspired by the richness of Kenyan food culture while embracing contemporary culinary ideas and international influences.
