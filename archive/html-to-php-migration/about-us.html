His Story

More Than A Chef. A Storyteller Through Food.

Food has the power to bring people together, create memories and tell stories. For Chef Ched Njogu, cooking is more than preparing a meal. It is about transforming quality ingredients into experiences that people remember.

Over the past seven years, Ched has developed his craft through hands-on culinary experience, continuous learning and a passion for creating food that combines flavour, presentation and personality. His approach is simple: understand the ingredients, respect the craft and create something that makes every guest feel special.

Today, Chef Ched is building a culinary brand focused on delivering exceptional food experiences while continuing to grow, innovate and inspire.

7 Years Of Culinary Experience

A Journey Built In The Kitchen

Seven years in the culinary industry have provided Chef Ched Njogu with the experience, discipline and perspective needed to understand what makes a successful food experience.

From the pressure of professional kitchens to the precision required to deliver an exceptional plate, every experience has contributed to the chef he is today.

But the journey is far from over.

Ched continues to learn, experiment and push his culinary creativity while building a brand that represents his passion for food and hospitality.
